xRETRO

Arcade classics, reimagined for the whole family. Pick a game with the stick, D-pad, arrow keys or TV remote, then press A or Enter.

Up to 4 players on one screen with Xbox or PlayStation controllers over Bluetooth, keyboard (arrows + Space, WASD + F) or touch, and more online: host a room in any game and share the code. Volume, music and resolution are in each game’s Settings.
